The New York Times, Anthropic, Bluesky, ByteDance, DeepMind, DeepSeek, OpenAI and Reddit artwork comes from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons). See simple-icons-license.txt. The Guardian, BBC and Hacker News tiles use local project lettermarks in their brand colours.

DeepMind and DeepSeek previously used lettermark placeholders; they now use the official brand marks. Tones are rendered in the site ink colour so the icons can invert in dark mode.
