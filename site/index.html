About the program

AI SentiWeather follows five AI companies — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, DeepSeek and ByteDance Seed — across news outlets (The New York Times, The Guardian, BBC) and online communities (Hacker News, Reddit, Bluesky).

Every day a scheduled job collects new items, scores their tone and republishes this site. Forum figures are samples; media figures use headlines and short excerpts.
